## Application checklist

Several positions can share sector, market or currency exposure. Assess aggregate risk and overlapping holding periods, not just each order in isolation. Compare experiments with comparable capital constraints and include portfolio drawdown and open positions.

Treat same-day and repeated-symbol observations as potentially dependent. Examine whether gains depend on one trade or market regime. Keep legitimate exceptional winners, but show concentration rather than assuming repeatability. Do not combine GBP and USD profit totals without a stated conversion basis.
