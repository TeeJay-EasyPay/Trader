## Decision principle

A distant target is not an expected return. Estimate probability-weighted outcomes, both trading legs, spread, slippage and forecast uncertainty. Missing cost assumptions are missing evidence, not zero cost. Keep broker charges separate from AI and hosting expense.

## Test

Compare unchanged selection with a precisely specified filter using the same future opportunities and execution assumptions. Keep skipped opportunities. Report net outcomes, risk and concentration, not just the reduction in commissions. Do not increase size to dilute percentage fees or hold a failing trade to avoid an exit charge.
