## Mechanics

Commission-free eligibility does not mean zero total cost. Regulatory fees and account/routing exceptions can apply. Funding, FX, ADR and financing costs are separate considerations. Reconcile posted fees; paper-account zeroes are not evidence of zero live charges.

## Test implications

Paper fills are simulated, not proof of live liquidity or execution. Use explicit cost/slippage scenarios, label uncertain fills, and compare matched broker records separately from the paired experiment. Unknown costs prevent a verified net-profit claim.
