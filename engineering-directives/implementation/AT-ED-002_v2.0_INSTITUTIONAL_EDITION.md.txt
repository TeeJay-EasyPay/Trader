# AI Trader Engineering Directive

# AT-ED-002 v2.0 -- Institutional Edition

# PART 1 -- Foundations

**Status:** Draft for Founder Review

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Executive Summary

This Engineering Directive defines the institutional foundations for the
next stage of AI Trader.

AI Trader is not being engineered as a collection of trading algorithms
or software modules. It is being engineered as a **continuously learning
Digital Investment Organisation** that combines research, governance,
engineering, portfolio management and operational excellence into a
single cohesive platform.

This directive establishes the philosophy, engineering standards,
institutional principles, deployment objectives and autonomous
operational model that will govern every subsequent implementation
decision.

The purpose of Part 1 is to ensure that every engineering decision
contributes to a platform that compounds capability over time rather
than accumulating disconnected features.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Founder Mission

The Founder has established the following strategic objectives:

1.  Deliver a robust, production-ready investment platform.
2.  Operate safely under strong governance.
3.  Continuously improve through evidence-based learning.
4.  Preserve institutional knowledge.
5.  Build reusable capabilities that support long-term evolution.
6.  Ensure every significant engineering decision can be understood,
    justified and audited.

This directive therefore places equal importance on software quality,
governance quality and organisational quality.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Engineering Manifesto

Every engineer, AI agent and implementation team participating in AI
Trader shall adopt the following philosophy.

## Build an Institution

Do not optimise for today's feature.

Optimise for the institution that will still be learning and improving
years from now.

## Knowledge Compounds

Knowledge is a permanent organisational asset.

Every experiment, implementation, success and failure must improve the
organisation.

Nothing valuable should be forgotten.

## Evidence Before Opinion

No strategy, architectural decision or operational change should rely
solely on opinion.

Evidence is the currency of engineering decisions.

## Governance Enables Autonomy

Autonomy is only valuable when bounded by governance.

The stronger the governance, the greater the confidence in autonomous
operation.

## Architecture Compounds

Every new capability should strengthen the overall platform.

Avoid isolated components, duplicated logic and fragmented ownership.

## Explainability Matters

Every recommendation, deployment and trading decision should be
explainable to the Founder.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Institutional Principles

1.  Founder authority is final.
2.  Capital preservation takes priority over aggressive optimisation.
3.  Risk has authority to stop unsafe behaviour.
4.  Learning is continuous.
5.  Research never ends.
6.  Strategies earn promotion through evidence.
7.  Engineering quality is never sacrificed for speed.
8.  Documentation is part of the product.
9.  Reusable organisational capability is preferred over tactical
    implementation.
10. Institutional memory must continuously grow.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

The engineering organisation shall prefer:

-   modular architecture
-   clear interfaces
-   low coupling
-   high cohesion
-   single sources of truth
-   observable systems
-   test-driven improvement
-   documentation-first governance
-   measurable outcomes

Temporary solutions should be clearly identified and scheduled for

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Architectural Principles

Maintain the following principles throughout implementation:

## Single Source of Truth

Critical business logic should exist in one authoritative location.

## Composability

Capabilities should be reusable across multiple workflows.

## Observability

Every significant process should generate meaningful telemetry.

## Auditability

Important decisions should be reconstructable from logs and reports.

## Security

Protect credentials, secrets, capital and operational integrity.

## Scalability

Design for future growth in assets, exchanges, strategies and users.

# Workstream 2 -- Autonomous Operations

## Philosophy

Operational autonomy exists to gather evidence and improve decision
making.

It must never bypass governance.

## Alpaca Paper Trading

Operate continuously using paper capital.

Objectives:

-   discover opportunities;
-   execute safely;
-   learn continuously;
-   improve strategies;
-   preserve complete audit trails.

## Kraken Governed Trading

Operate only within existing Founder-approved constraints.

Maintain:

-   exposure limits;
-   trade-size limits;
-   daily loss limits;
-   emergency stop capability;
-   complete audit history.

Purpose:

Gain operational experience while protecting capital.

# Risks and Assumptions

## Assumptions

-   Existing repository architecture remains authoritative.
-   Current governance remains valid unless intentionally superseded.
-   Hosted infrastructure is available.
-   Existing integrations remain functional.

## Key Risks

-   Deployment defects.
-   Integration failures.
-   External API instability.
-   Model overfitting.
-   Strategy degradation.
-   Documentation drift.
-   Governance inconsistencies.

Document any newly identified risks.

architecture unless evidence demonstrates a clear engineering benefit.

Avoid speculative optimisation.

Avoid unnecessary technology changes.

# Purpose

This part defines the architecture, organisational capabilities and
engineering expectations for the Autonomous Investment Intelligence
Platform (AIIP).

The objective is not to create isolated artificial intelligence
features. The objective is to engineer a coordinated Digital Investment
Organisation capable of continuously improving its investment
decision-making through research, experimentation, validation,
governance and institutional learning.

Every capability should strengthen the organisation as a whole.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Strategic Objectives

The platform shall:

-   Discover investment opportunities.
-   Generate evidence-based hypotheses.
-   Validate every hypothesis scientifically.
-   Learn continuously from outcomes.
-   Improve portfolio quality over time.
-   Preserve institutional knowledge.
-   Operate within governance defined in Part 3.
-   Produce transparent recommendations for Founder oversight.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Research Division

## Mission

Continuously increase the organisation's investment knowledge while
maintaining evidence quality and traceability.

## Research Domains

Implement coordinated research capabilities for:

-   Quantitative Research
-   Technical Analysis
-   Fundamental Analysis
-   Macroeconomic Analysis
-   Market Structure
-   Alternative Data
-   Behavioural Finance
-   Portfolio Theory
-   Execution Quality
-   Risk Research

## Responsibilities

The Research Division shall:

-   identify promising ideas;
-   analyse market behaviour;
-   study academic literature where appropriate;
-   review broker and exchange documentation;
-   evaluate public educational resources;
-   identify research gaps;
-   create reusable knowledge assets.

All research shall be attributable to its source and stored within the
institutional knowledge base.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Strategy Discovery Division

## Mission

Generate, refine and prioritise investment hypotheses.

## Inputs

-   Research findings
-   Historical data
-   Live market observations
-   Existing strategies
-   Portfolio performance
-   Lessons learned
-   Founder priorities

## Responsibilities

Discover:

-   new strategies;
-   execution improvements;
-   indicator combinations;
-   parameter optimisation opportunities;
-   diversification opportunities;
-   portfolio enhancements.

Every strategy shall receive a unique Strategy ID and lifecycle record.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Strategy Laboratory

## Mission

Provide a controlled environment for objective evaluation.

Every strategy shall complete an identical validation pipeline.

### Stage 1

Concept Review

### Stage 2

Implementation Review

### Stage 3

Historical Backtesting

### Stage 4

Walk-Forward Validation

### Stage 5

Monte Carlo Analysis

### Stage 6

Market Regime Validation

### Stage 7

Transaction Cost Analysis

### Stage 8

Slippage Analysis

### Stage 9

Portfolio Contribution Analysis

### Stage 10

Confidence Assessment

Strategies failing objective quality thresholds shall not progress.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Validation Office

The Validation Office is independent of the Research Division.

Its purpose is to challenge assumptions rather than confirm them.

Responsibilities include:

-   reproducibility;
-   robustness testing;
-   sensitivity analysis;
-   overfitting detection;
-   survivorship bias review;
-   data leakage detection;
-   implementation verification.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Portfolio Management Office

The platform shall optimise portfolios rather than individual trades.

Evaluate:

-   expected return;
-   diversification;
-   concentration;
-   exposure;
-   sector allocation;
-   currency allocation;
-   correlation;
-   volatility;
-   liquidity;
-   drawdown.

Recommendations should consider portfolio impact before individual trade
attractiveness.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Risk Office

Risk evaluation is continuous.

Assess:

-   market risk;
-   liquidity risk;
-   execution risk;
-   operational risk;
-   concentration risk;
-   counterparty risk;
-   model risk;
-   technology risk.

Risk assessments accompany every promotion recommendation.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Execution Office

Translate approved strategies into executable trading behaviour.

Responsibilities include:

-   broker orchestration;
-   order validation;
-   execution monitoring;
-   reconciliation;
-   exception handling;
-   audit logging;
-   operational metrics.

Execution quality shall be measured independently from strategy quality.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Performance Office

Measure organisational effectiveness.

Track:

-   prediction accuracy;
-   execution quality;
-   strategy performance;
-   portfolio performance;
-   research productivity;
-   learning velocity;
-   innovation rate;
-   risk-adjusted returns;
-   drawdown recovery;
-   capital efficiency.

Performance metrics shall drive continuous improvement.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Learning Engine

The Learning Engine coordinates organisational improvement.

Inputs include:

-   research;
-   experiments;
-   historical testing;
-   paper trading;
-   live observations;
-   portfolio outcomes.

Responsibilities:

-   detect strategy decay;
-   detect parameter drift;
-   identify regime transitions;
-   compare expected versus actual outcomes;
-   recommend improvements;
-   identify research priorities.

Learning recommendations require supporting evidence.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Knowledge Management Office

Knowledge is treated as a permanent institutional asset.

Maintain:

-   research catalogue;
-   experiment register;
-   strategy register;
-   lessons learned;
-   architecture decisions;
-   validation reports;
-   performance reports.

Select analytical techniques according to the problem being solved.

The platform may employ statistical methods, optimisation techniques,

Avoid adopting techniques solely because they are fashionable.

-   Why did it happen?
-   Was the outcome expected?
-   What evidence supports the conclusion?
-   What should the organisation change?
-   What knowledge has been gained?

Every iteration should leave the organisation stronger than before.

Trader.

This part establishes the governance framework for the Autonomous
Investment Intelligence Platform.

Governance exists to ensure that research, strategy development,
